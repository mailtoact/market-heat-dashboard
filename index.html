How the score works

The score combines five buckets: rates/term premium, labor, credit, oil/inflation pressure, and DXY/liquidity. Each bucket uses both the current level and recent direction where appropriate. Credit and labor receive more weight because they are more useful for detecting a transition toward recession. The model is intentionally transparent and tunable in fetch_data.py.

Informational monitoring tool only; not investment advice. Data may be delayed or revised. FRED is the primary source for Treasury, unemployment, oil and credit series. DXY is sourced from Yahoo Finance. Historical values and macro releases can be revised.
